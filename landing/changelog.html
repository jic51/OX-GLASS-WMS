What's new

Every change that reaches your installation, newest first. Support and updates are the same subscription — when something here ships, you get it.

August 2026

A full audit of the app, and the first fixes out of it: the evening date, the quantity that was not whole, and a check that counts every door before each release. Plus the movement screens rebuilt as one, packs, and backups that carry your settings.

v11.32 Fixed

A highlight you can actually finish reading

When the app points you at the movements a notice is talking about, it marks them in yellow. Six seconds is plenty for one or two. For eight, the colour was gone before you had scrolled to the third.

The mark now lasts in proportion to how much there is to read — six seconds for one, half a minute at the most — and it holds its colour before fading instead of washing out from the first moment. Past a handful it also tells you how many are marked, so you are not counting highlights while you scroll.

v11.31 Fixed

The links on those notices now go somewhere

Two things you could press on a system notice did nothing useful. Show the movements answered "those rows are in the archived history — use Load Older History", which was true every single time and helped no one: the app knew which rows were missing and knew how to fetch them. It fetches them now, and takes you straight there.

Details opened Settings and highlighted nothing. The highlight was real, but it was looking for the entry before the page had finished loading it, and gave up without a word. It now waits for the page instead of guessing how long the page will take.

v11.30 Fixed

The app was doing its nightly work and never told you

Backups ran every night. Old movements were archived, stock totals were rebuilt, and every one of those was recorded — 27 backups are sitting in Drive right now. But the corner of the screen never showed a card saying so, and Settings → System said nothing automatic had ever run. Both were reading the same list, and that list arrived empty every single time.

One character in one line: the server asked for the signed-in user by a name that does not exist at that point, the error was caught and turned into an empty list, and nothing was ever written down about it. Nothing was lost and nothing was broken — the record simply never reached the screen. It does now, and the error that hid it can no longer hide.

v11.29 Fixed

The window that opened behind the window

From the morning arrivals list, pressing Mark arrived opened the edit window behind it — and the form underneath could not be touched at all. The only way forward was to close the list and lose your place in it.

The app deliberately makes every window but the front one inactive, so a click can never land on a half-hidden form. It was doing that correctly; the morning list had simply been told it was in front of everything, including the windows it opens itself. It now sits behind what it opens.

v11.29 Fixed

A renamed role is now called by its name everywhere

If you rename the warehouse role — to Supervisor, say — the account panel showed the new name on the profile card and the old internal one in the Active Users row directly beneath it. Same person, same panel, two different words.

Every screen that shows a role now uses your name for it. The one exception is deliberate: the first-time setup wizard still says Warehouse, because it runs before there is a custom name to use.

v11.28 Security

What a role may not see is no longer sent to it

Cost figures were reaching every browser and then being hidden by the page that drew them. That is not a permission — it is a preference, and it is not what the feature page promises. Unit cost, total cost and the running average now leave the server only for roles allowed to see them: administrators always, the warehouse role when an administrator has turned See costs on, and read-only users never. Nothing looks different on screen; the difference is that the numbers were never handed over.

The same load was also sending the old list of colleagues' email addresses with their roles beside it, and the owner's address, to every role on every load — in service of nothing, since no screen in the app has read that list since user management moved to its own page. Administrators still get the real list; everyone else no longer gets a staff roster they never asked for.

v11.28 Fixed

A category with a < in it no longer cuts a screen in half

Category names, attached file names and units were being placed into the page without being treated as plain text. A category typed as GLASS <5MM ended the screen right there — no error message, just a page that stopped, and a person reasonably assuming their data was gone.

Categories go through one shared piece of the app, so this was one correction across seven screens: the stock table, the movement list, packs, the material picker, the low-stock monitor and both expected delivery views.

v11.27 Fixed

After six in the evening, the app was writing tomorrow's date

A delivery received at 6:30pm on the 14th was recorded as the 15th. Not rejected, not flagged — filed one day late, every evening, by the app itself. The date box on the movement window opened already filled in that way, so unless somebody noticed and changed it, that is the date that was saved. Month-end totals were off by whatever crossed the dock after six.

The app was reading the clock in UTC instead of in your own time zone. It now reads the day you are actually standing in, everywhere — the movement form, the week's arrivals, the file names on exports. Dates already in your sheet are untouched; correct any evening entries you want to fix with Adjust or by editing the movement.

v11.27 Fixed

Half a sheet is a real quantity, and it was being rounded away

Typing 2.5 into a quantity box saved 2. Typing 0.75 saved nothing at all. No warning appeared, so the only way to catch it was to go back and read the row afterwards. Anyone working in SQ FT, LN FT, sheets or rolls was affected — the units that exist precisely because material comes in halves.

Quantity boxes now accept decimals and keep them, from the entry form through to the sheet. Zero is still refused, with the app saying so in its own words. Whole numbers behave exactly as before.

v11.27 Security

One page of the system's own activity was readable without signing in

The list of what the system did on its own — backups, maintenance, who saved what and when — could be read by someone who had your app's link and a Google account, without the app ever checking who they were. It exposed no stock figures, no costs and no material data, and there is no sign it was ever read. It is closed.

What matters more than the one page: there is now an automatic check that counts every way into the system before each release and refuses to ship if any one of them is left unlocked or unexplained. This gap existed because nothing was counting. Something is now.

v11.25 – v11.26 Fixed

Expected deliveries: the category that disappeared, and the window that would not come back

Opening a delivery to edit it showed the category box empty even though one had been chosen — and saving from there wrote that emptiness back. The cause was a rename: renaming a category updated your movements and your history but left expected deliveries pointing at the old name. It now renames them too, and a delivery whose category is no longer on your list says so instead of silently blanking.

The morning list of arrivals also closed for good once you marked the first item, with a full page reload the only way back. It reopens from This week's arrivals in the account menu, where everyone can reach it rather than only on the Incoming screen. Every item carries its own Mark arrived button, and the layout puts the quantity beside the name with the status underneath it.

v11.24 Fixed

An expected delivery no longer arrives pre-filled with a 1

The quantity box on a new expected delivery opened with a 1 in it, which is a number to plan around whether or not anyone meant it. It opens empty, like every other quantity box in the app.

Leaving it empty is allowed on purpose: a delivery entered from an attached loading sheet often has no count until the truck arrives. Where a quantity was never stated, the app says qty not stated rather than printing a zero.

v11.23 New

Check my data — the same thing written two ways, found for you

Type a supplier's name slightly differently twice and you have two suppliers. Nothing breaks; the totals simply split in half and the reports quietly stop adding up. Check my data, in Settings, reads your whole archive and shows what it found: the same name spelt two ways, materials missing a supplier or a project, and names close enough to each other to be a typo.

The first two come with a Merge button that does the work across every affected row at once. The third — possible typos — deliberately does not: JJF 109 and JJF 110 look almost identical to a computer and are two different jobs. That one is for your eyes only.

v11.22 New

Correcting a count is not the same as recording waste

The app says 40 and the rack holds 38. That does not mean two broke — it means the record is wrong, and filing it as waste would quietly turn your waste figure into "waste plus counting mistakes". Those are two different problems with two different fixes.

Adjust is a movement type of its own, on the movement window and on the rack drawer. You type what you counted, not the difference — the app knows what it believed and does the subtraction — and it tells you what will happen before you save. It costs nothing, it belongs to no job, it needs a reason, and it can go up as easily as down.

v11.21 Fixed

An expected delivery keeps the status you gave it

Entering a delivery as already Arrived, or cancelling one on the spot, saved it as Pending anyway and needed a second edit to stick. Creating and editing disagreed about the same field; they go through one place now.

Deleting one also said nothing at all while it worked, so pressing again was the reasonable thing to do — and the second press hit a row that was already gone and reported an error for a delete that had succeeded. The button now shows it is working, a second press is ignored, and a delivery that is already gone ends as success.

v11.17 New

Editing a movement fills in what the app already knows

A transfer or a write-off is saved with supplier, contract, PO and project empty, because those boxes are hidden on those screens. Open one to correct something and you used to face five blanks for a material whose supplier the app has known all along.

Those boxes now arrive filled from that same material's own history, most recent first — and every suggested one is marked, so you can always see which values are recorded on that movement and which the app is offering. Anything the row already has is never touched.

v11.16 Fixed

A transfer no longer invents a project

Moving material between two racks is not a change of job, but a transfer was being stamped GENERIC — the app's word for "in stock, unassigned" — because the project box is hidden on that screen and nobody filled it in. Filter a material's history and the same job looked like two different stories.

A transfer now keeps the job the material already had, and the app never asserts something it was not told.

v11.13 – v11.15 Safety

Your backup carries your settings — and says so when it can't

A backup copies your spreadsheet, but part of an installation lives outside it. Restore a copy without that part and everything looks right until an attachment refuses to open, months later, with nothing pointing at why.

Every backup now has a tab inside it listing exactly what to put back and what each one is for, in plain words — including the handful that must be replaced with new values after a restore rather than copied. If it cannot be written, the backup says so on the spot instead of looking complete.

v11.12 New

The Terms and the Privacy Policy can be read before you accept them

The consent box asked you to agree to two documents you had no way to open. Both now live on their own tabs inside your own file, linked from the box itself, and they stay there afterwards — so the version you agreed to is always the version you can go back and read.

v11.5 – v11.11 New

The app remembers how many come in a box

The first time you receive a material by the box, case, pallet or bag, it asks once: 1 box of GE SILPRUF = how many units? After that it just says 12 units per box, with an edit link, and it does the arithmetic — including the cost per unit, updated as you type.

Not every material comes in a box and not every box holds the same number, so nothing is assumed: a material with no answer on record is simply counted in units, as before. The whole list is editable in Settings → Materials.

v11.9 Fixed

A cost label that could have made a box price look like a unit price

For four versions, choosing Box as the unit relabelled the cost field "Cost per Box" while nothing divided it — so a price typed for a box of twelve went into the running average as the price of one unit. Cost is worked out as a weighted average, so a wrong figure there affects every later cost for that material, not just the one movement.

The field now names a pack only when the app actually knows how many are in it, and divides by exactly that number. If you priced anything by the box between v11.5 and v11.8, it is worth checking that material's cost.

v11.6 – v11.8 New

The five movement screens are one screen now

Entry, exit, transfer, return and waste had drifted into five different layouts — fields in different orders, quantity boxes in different places, and a "1" already sitting in the quantity on three of them.

They now share one shape: the material in a neutral box, the movement's colour only around the part that says where and how many, nothing filled in when the window opens, and the cursor in the name field ready to type. Switching between them keeps what you have already written instead of making you type it again.

v11.3 – v11.4 Fixed

Two small things that were quietly wrong

Un-ticking the consent box left the consent recorded. And on a delivery with several materials, autofill filled the first line rather than the line you were working on.

v10.8 New

Your own AI key, set from inside the app

Reading a supplier's confirmation email into your expected deliveries runs on a key you own, entered in Settings — not one of ours. It works on your quota, at your cost, and can be removed at any time.

v11.18 – v11.20 Terms

What support covers, and how billing works, is written down

Section 5 of the Terms now says what the subscription includes, how fast an email is answered, what is quoted separately, and what you are expected to do. Section 9 covers cancelling, refunds, how invoices are sent, and what happens if a payment does not go through — including the one thing worth knowing in advance: your data is never withheld over a payment, and the app keeps working.

v10.0 – v10.4 Safety

Two people can change the same thing at once without losing one of them

Stock is worked out from your movement history, so any change that reads it, adjusts it and writes it back can collide with another one happening at the same second — and the old failure was silent: both people saw "saved", and the number was quietly wrong.

Saving a movement was already protected. Now editing a saved movement, renaming a category, renaming or merging a material, and merging projects, suppliers or locations are too. If two changes ever do meet, the second one is told to try again in a moment instead of overwriting the first.

v10.5 New

See how much room your file has left

Settings → System now shows a bar with how much of your spreadsheet is used, and roughly how many years of room is left at your current pace. The estimate is measured over your last 90 days and doesn't appear until you have 60 days of history — your first weeks are mostly the opening inventory, and projecting from those would be wrong.

v10.1 – v10.6 Fixed

Renaming a category now updates everything, straight away

Renaming a category updates every movement that used it, both the recent ones and the archived ones, and the whole app shows the new name immediately instead of after a reload. The same fix reached merging locations, projects and suppliers.

Renaming used to take minutes on a busy warehouse with no sign it was working. It now takes seconds, and the button says what it is doing.

v10.3 Fixed

Settings says which renames carry into your history

Renaming a category updates your existing movements. Renaming a supplier, project or location does not — those are records of what was true at the time, and they're already printed on paperwork that went out. Every tab now says which one it is, instead of one sentence that was only true on one of them.

v9.93 – v9.95 New

Every backup, listed in one place

Settings → System lists every backup that actually exists in your Drive folder, read from Drive itself. Backups also now carry a copy of your configuration inside them, so restoring one brings back your settings and not just your data.

v9.94 New

Mark a delivery as arrived from the morning popup

The "Arriving today" card now takes you straight to the right place to record it. Admins only.

v9.87 New

A warning when a price moves

Enter a unit cost noticeably different from what you have been paying and the app says so before you save, so a typo in a purchase price doesn't quietly become your average cost.

v9.78 – v9.81 New

Costs: what your stock is worth, per project and in waste

Optional weighted-average costing. Enter what you paid on the way in and the app tracks the average, shows the cost of what has gone out to each project, and puts a dollar figure on waste. Every cost is behind its own permission — the people recording movements don't have to see money.

v9.76 – v9.79 New

Decide what the warehouse role can do — and what to call it

Permissions are now switches rather than a fixed wall: give the warehouse role the ability to edit movements or manage the catalog if that is how your shop runs. And if you call them Receivers or Yard, rename the role to match.

Earlier

The foundations, before the versions above.

v9.82 New

The rack drawer, rebuilt

Tap a location on the Warehouse Map and act on what's in it — exit, transfer or waste — with the material, category and source rack already filled in. Tap the "available" figure to fill the quantity.

v9.73 Fixed

Usable with a thumb

A full pass over every screen at phone and tablet sizes: nothing runs off the edge, nothing is too small to tap, no screen scrolls sideways. A warehouse is not run from a desk.

v9.74 New

Low-stock alerts you actually set up

Choose which materials matter and what the minimum is. The dashboard tells you what is below it and what has hit zero.

v9.71 New

Backups you can run and check from inside the app

Turn the nightly backup on, run one now, and see when the last one happened — without opening the spreadsheet or the script editor.

v9.61 New

Paste a supplier email, get the expected deliveries

Paste the confirmation you were sent and the app pulls out what is coming, how much, and when — into the Incoming list, for you to check before saving.

v9.58 New

A health check for the parts you can't see

One command that inspects the half of an installation nobody looks at — folders, permissions, scheduled jobs — repairs what it safely can, and tells you plainly what is missing.
