What it does

Built by someone who runs a warehouse.

Acopio started as the system for a window-installation shop's own floor, and it is still in use every day. Everything here exists because a real shift needed it.

  • In, out, transfers, returns and waste — every movement tied to a person, a job and a rack.
  • Stock by rack location, so "where is it" has an answer before anyone walks.
  • Low-stock alerts on the materials you choose, at the level you set.
  • Cost per job — weighted average cost, stamped on each movement so last year's numbers stay last year's.
  • Incoming deliveries, with a morning summary of what's arriving today.
  • Photos and PDFs attached to any movement, filed privately in your Drive.
  • Roles and permissions — decide who sees costs, who can edit history, who's read-only.
  • Nightly backups to your own Drive, kept 30 days.

What it doesn't do yet

  • Barcode scanning and label printing
  • Cycle counting
  • Accounting or e-commerce integrations
  • A native phone app — it's a web app, and works on a phone

We'd rather you read this here than find it in the demo. If any of these are essential to you today, Acopio isn't the right fit yet — and we'll say so on the call.

From the founder

Why this exists.

JC
Jose Castro Founder · Warehouse Manager at a window-installation shop

I run a warehouse. Not a software company that studied warehouses — an actual floor, with actual racks, where being wrong about what's on a shelf costs a crew half a morning.

I know what it costs to walk out and check because the spreadsheet can't be trusted. I know what it's like to find out on a Friday that the material for Monday's job left last week and nobody wrote it down.

Acopio exists because I needed it and nothing fit. The serious systems cost more than the problem and take months to roll out. The cheap ones want your team to abandon the tools they already know. Neither was built for a company our size.

So we built it to sit somewhere unusual: your inventory lives in your Google Drive, not ours. We don't host it. We don't store it. We can't read it. There is no Acopio database with your suppliers and your prices in it, because there is no Acopio database.

People ask what's left to hold onto, if we hold nothing at all. The answer is the part that matters: the commitment.

We can't hold your data hostage, because we never have it. If you stop paying, nothing gets switched off — your system keeps running and your history stays yours. That means we have to earn the next month, every month, on the work alone. It's a harder way to build a business. It's also the only way I'd want a supplier of mine to work.

You're not buying a login. You're buying somebody who has stood where you're standing, and who has to keep being useful to stay.
