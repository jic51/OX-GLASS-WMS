Your categories, your rack names, your team, your suppliers — configured with you on a call, not left as homework. You get a working system, not a login and good luck.

What it does

Built by someone who runs a warehouse.

Acopio started as the system for a glass shop's own floor, and it still runs one every day. Everything here exists because a real shift needed it.

  • In, out, transfers, returns and waste — every movement tied to a person, a job and a rack.
  • Stock by rack location, so "where is it" has an answer before anyone walks.
  • Low-stock alerts on the materials you choose, at the level you set.
  • Cost per job — weighted average cost, stamped on each movement so last year's numbers stay last year's.
  • Incoming deliveries, with a morning summary of what's arriving today.
  • Photos and PDFs attached to any movement, filed privately in your Drive.
  • Roles and permissions — decide who sees costs, who can edit history, who's read-only.
  • Nightly backups to your own Drive, kept 30 days.

What it doesn't do yet

  • Barcode scanning and label printing
  • Cycle counting
  • Accounting or e-commerce integrations
  • A native phone app — it's a web app, and works on a phone

We'd rather you read this here than find it in the demo. If any of these are essential to you today, Acopio isn't the right fit yet — and we'll say so on the call.
